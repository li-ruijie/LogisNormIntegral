Part 1: pointwise accuracy at six (eta, v) configurations
=========================================================================
  - 'truth' = adaptive Gauss-Kronrod (`integrate`), cross-checked against
    500-node Gauss-Hermite quadrature (agreement asserted to <= 1e-13).
    The original 100-node GH reference had ~6e-11 error at v = 9, which
    is larger than the K = 17 minimax bound Delta_17 = 2.5e-12 and would
    thus measure the *reference's* error, not the minimax error.
  - K = 8, 11, 17 are the minimax mixtures shipped with this package.
  - MC M = 10^2, 10^4, 10^6 are i.i.d. Monte Carlo estimates of E_M[Lambda].

Part 2: quasi-Bayesian Monte Carlo for the Natural Indirect Effect (NIE)
=========================================================================
  Synthetic mediation data (n = 500): treat -> mediator -> binary outcome.
  Generated from known true coefficients so we have two reference values:
    NIE_pop:  population NIE from the true data-generating process
              (high-precision quadrature on the true coefficients).
    NIE_hat:  data-conditional NIE the QB methods should estimate
              (high-precision quadrature on the fitted coefficients).
  All QB methods evaluate the same NIE. Only the *inner* integral differs.
  Three outcome-prevalence scenarios (theta_0 = -3, -1, 0) cover low,
  moderate, and high marginal P(Y=1) (~0.38, 0.69, 0.82). The V&VW
  remainder behaves differently in each regime because |Lambda^(4)|
  depends on the linear predictor.

Notes:
  - All methods estimate NIE_hat (the data-conditional posterior target).
  - 'bias_vs_hat' = QB posterior mean - NIE_hat.
      * Minimax K = 22, 17, 11: inner-integral error is bounded by each
        Delta_K^* (3.6e-15 to 1.3e-11), invisible against QB outer noise.
      * V&VW: O(v^2) Taylor bias that does not vanish with N_qb.
      * Inner MC: O(sigma_Lambda / sqrt(M_inner)) noise per draw, which
        averages out as N_qb -> infty but inflates CI width at small M_inner.
  - All three minimax variants (K = 22, 17, 11) agree to >= 10 decimals
    everywhere, so the cert hierarchy is below QB sampling resolution.
  - The V&VW Taylor bias is regime-dependent, flipping sign from negative
    at low prevalence to positive at moderate and high, and largest in
    magnitude at high outcome prevalence.
  - For interactive use with N_qb >= 10^4 outer draws, the certified
    minimax mixture is the only practical choice. It is as fast as V&VW
    but unbiased, and more than 380x faster than inner MC at M_inner = 10^4.
    That figure is a floor against the K = 22 default, this script's
    timing reference. The measured ratios in this run are 597x, 478x,
    and 399x, so quote the floor as a floor.
