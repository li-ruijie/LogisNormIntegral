  Target = fixed population NIE (true coefficients), contrast z = 1,
  z' : 0 -> 1, covariate c = 0. Inner integral: certified minimax K = 11.
  The NIE odds ratio is covered by the mixture and by the rare-outcome
  closed form of Valeri and VanderWeele, off identical outer draws.

Notes:
  - Coverage is the fraction of the N_sim intervals containing the fixed
    population NIE. SE is the binomial Monte Carlo error sqrt(p(1-p)/N).
  - The minimax QB interval propagates full parameter uncertainty through
    the exact nonlinear map, so coverage tracks the nominal 95% level.
  - The rare-outcome closed form estimates exp{(theta_M + theta_TM) gamma_T},
    which equals the NIE odds ratio only in the rare-outcome limit. None of
    the three regimes is rare, so its intervals centre on a different
    quantity and seldom cover the exact odds ratio.
