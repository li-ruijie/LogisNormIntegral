=========================================================================
  n = 500 per data set, N_sim = 2000 data sets, R_qb = 4000 outer draws.
  Target = fixed population NIE (true coefficients), contrast z = 1,
  z' : 0 -> 1, covariate c = 0. Inner integral: certified minimax K = 11
  vs Valeri--VanderWeele 2nd-order Taylor, off identical outer draws.

Notes:
  - Coverage is the fraction of the N_sim intervals containing the fixed
    population NIE. SE is the binomial Monte Carlo error sqrt(p(1-p)/N).
  - The minimax QB interval propagates full parameter uncertainty through
    the exact nonlinear map, so coverage tracks the nominal 95% level.
  - V&VW's O(v^2) Taylor bias shifts its intervals off the target. The
    resulting mis-coverage flips direction with outcome prevalence (severe
    under-coverage at low, over-coverage at moderate, and under-coverage at
    high), matching the sign-flipping bias in Example1.R and the manuscript
    method-comparison table.
